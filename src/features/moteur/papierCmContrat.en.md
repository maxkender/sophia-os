## RECITALS

**A.** The Company publishes the mobile application *Sophia: General Knowledge* (the "**Application**") and promotes it internationally through a set of branded social media personas operated on Instagram and TikTok.

**B.** The Company wishes to engage the Contractor to create, operate and publish content on social media accounts bearing those personas, in the language and cultural context of the Contractor's market.

**C.** The Parties have entered into a service contract on the Upwork platform (the "**Upwork Contract**"). This Agreement supplements the Upwork Contract and governs ownership of the accounts, the credentials and the content.

**D.** It is the express and essential intention of both Parties that the Company is, and has been from the moment of their creation, the sole owner of all such accounts, and that the Contractor holds no ownership interest in them at any time.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Accounts"** means all social media, messaging, analytics and related third-party accounts created, operated or administered by the Contractor in connection with the Services, including without limitation those listed in **Schedule A**, together with any account subsequently created under or in connection with the Personas.

**1.2 "Brand Assets"** means the Personas, the name "Sophia", the title *Sophia: General Knowledge*, and any national or linguistic variation thereof, the Company's trademarks, logos, visual identity, tone of voice, templates and brand guidelines.

**1.3 "Content"** means all material created, produced, captured, edited, commissioned or published by the Contractor in connection with the Services, including posts, captions, scripts, videos, images, audio, graphics, hashtag sets, editorial calendars and account biographies.

**1.4 "Credentials"** means, for each Account and each Linked Email Address: the username and handle, the password, the associated email address and its own password, the recovery email address, the recovery phone number, the two-factor authentication method together with its seed, secret key or QR code, and the full set of backup or recovery codes.

**1.5 "Linked Email Address"** means each email address created by or for the Contractor for the purpose of registering, recovering or administering an Account.

**1.6 "Personas"** means the branded identities under which the Accounts are operated, including `sophia_espagne`, `sophia_france`, `sophia_deutschland`, `sophia_usa` and any other identity designated by the Company from time to time, in any country and any language.

**1.7 "Services"** means the services described in **Schedule B** and in the Upwork Contract.

## 3. CREATION AND OWNERSHIP OF THE ACCOUNTS

**3.1 Creation as agent.** Where the Contractor creates any Account or Linked Email Address, it does so **exclusively in the name of, on behalf of and for the sole benefit of the Company**, as the Company's agent and nominee. The Company is the sole owner of each Account and each Linked Email Address **from the moment of its creation**. No ownership interest, beneficial interest, goodwill or other proprietary right vests in the Contractor at any time, and no transfer of ownership from the Contractor to the Company is required or intended.

**3.2 Custodian only.** The Contractor holds the Accounts, the Linked Email Addresses and the Credentials solely as custodian, for the account of the Company, and acknowledges that it has no lien, retention right, set-off right or other security interest over any of them, including in the event of any dispute over payment or performance.

**3.3 Naming and registration details.** The Contractor shall create each Account and Linked Email Address using exactly the handle, display name, email address, recovery details and profile information specified in writing by the Company. The Contractor shall not register any Account or email address under or in connection with the Personas other than those instructed by the Company.

**3.4 Delivery of Credentials.** The Contractor shall transmit the complete Credentials for each Account and each Linked Email Address to the Company **within twenty-four (24) hours of creation**, by the secure channel designated by the Company, and shall keep them accurate and up to date at all times thereafter.

**3.5 No unilateral change.** The Contractor shall not change, disable, remove or add any password, email address, recovery email address, recovery phone number, two-factor authentication method, backup code, linked account, connected application or administrator without the Company's prior written consent. Any such change made without consent shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

**3.6 Immediate takeover.** The Company may at any time, with or without cause and without notice, assume full and exclusive control of any Account, change any Credentials, revoke any access granted to the Contractor, or require the Contractor to perform any act necessary to give effect to such takeover. The Contractor shall cooperate immediately and shall not obstruct, delay or contest such takeover.

**3.7 Business Manager.** Where a platform offers a business or organisation-level container (including Meta Business Suite, TikTok Business Center or equivalent), the Contractor shall promptly assign each Account to the container designated by the Company and shall accept a limited, revocable role therein.

**3.8 Power of attorney.** The Contractor irrevocably authorises the Company to take any step, and to sign any document, declaration or platform form in the Contractor's name, that may be necessary to confirm, register, recover or enforce the Company's ownership of the Accounts and the Linked Email Addresses. This authority survives termination of this Agreement.

**3.9 Platform claims.** The Contractor shall not file, and irrevocably waives any right to file, any ownership claim, appeal, recovery request, counter-notice or complaint with any platform in respect of any Account, and shall not report any Account as compromised, hacked or impersonating.

## 5. DATA, SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

**5.1 Security.** The Contractor shall store all Credentials in the password manager designated by the Company (Bitwarden), shall not record them in plain text, and shall not transmit them by any channel other than the one designated by the Company. The Contractor shall not access the Accounts from shared, public or unsecured devices or networks.

**5.2 No sharing.** The Contractor shall not disclose the Credentials to, or permit access to any Account by, any third party, including any subcontractor, employee or family member, without the Company's prior written consent.

**5.3 Incident notification.** The Contractor shall notify the Company **within twelve (12) hours** of becoming aware of any loss, theft, unauthorised access, suspension, restriction, strike, appeal or platform enforcement action affecting any Account or Linked Email Address.

**5.4 Platform rules and law.** The Contractor shall comply with the terms of service and community guidelines of each platform, and with all applicable law on advertising, consumer protection and the disclosure of commercial content. The Contractor shall not purchase followers, engagement or reviews, use bots or automation tools not approved by the Company, or engage in any practice liable to result in the suspension of an Account.

**5.5 Personal data.** Where the Contractor processes personal data on behalf of the Company, it shall do so only on the Company's documented instructions, shall keep such data confidential, shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, and shall delete or return such data on termination. The Parties shall enter into a separate data processing agreement where required by applicable data protection law.

## 7. TERM, TERMINATION AND HANDOVER

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for the duration of the Upwork Contract, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 7.

**7.2 Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on fourteen (14) days' written notice.

**7.3 Termination for breach.** The Company may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect on written notice if the Contractor breaches Clause 3, Clause 4, Clause 5 or Clause 6.

**7.4 Handover.** On termination for any reason, and in any event **within forty-eight (48) hours**, the Contractor shall:

- **(a)** deliver to the Company the complete and current Credentials for every Account and Linked Email Address;
- **(b)** transfer all working files, source files, editorial calendars, drafts and analytics exports in their original editable formats;
- **(c)** perform any act required to transfer administration of each Account and Linked Email Address to the Company, including accepting or confirming any platform transfer request;
- **(d)** permanently remove the Accounts from all of its own devices, applications and password managers, and delete all copies of the Credentials in its possession;
- **(e)** cease all use of the Brand Assets; and
- **(f)** confirm completion of the above in writing.

**7.5 No retention.** The Contractor shall perform its obligations under Clause 7.4 in full **regardless of any dispute**, including any dispute as to fees due, and shall not condition handover on payment.

**7.6 Survival.** Clauses 3, 4, 5.5, 6, 7.4, 8, 9, 10 and 11 survive termination.
